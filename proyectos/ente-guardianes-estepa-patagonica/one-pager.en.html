The project

Twelve ranching families steward 61,346 hectares of steppe on private land within the Somuncurá Plateau Protected Natural Area. Regenerative grazing management restores native bunchgrass, improves soil health and enables organic-carbon capture, with social and biodiversity co-benefits (CCB Standard) and independent satellite monitoring (MRV) by ForestBlock. The project has approved provincial environmental feasibility and is advancing toward soil sampling (INTA), Verra registration and verification.
